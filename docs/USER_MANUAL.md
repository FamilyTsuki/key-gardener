## 1. Account Management (Mandatory)

To experience the full game and community features, creating a profile is required.

### Registration and Login
- Navigate to the homepage and click on **Register**.
- Fill in the required fields (Username, Email, Password). 
- Once your account is created, use the **Login** tab to access your secure session. This unlocks your cloud saves and the Community Hub.

### Forgot Password
- If you lose your credentials, click on "Forgot Password?" on the login page.
- Enter your email address to receive a secure, encrypted link allowing you to reset your password instantly.

## 3. The Game: Launch and Mechanics

### Starting a Game
1. Click **Play** from the homepage or navigate to the **Saves** menu.
2. The system offers **3 distinct save slots** (synced to the cloud via your account).
3. Select a slot to resume your progress or start a fresh descent into the network.

### How to Play? (The 3 Phases)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The game is entirely controlled via the **keyboard**. The mouse is completely useless once the game starts.

The gameplay is divided into three distinct phases:

#### 1. Exploration Phase (The Corridor)
- The floor is made of tiles representing your physical keyboard.
- **Action**: Strike the corresponding key on your real keyboard to move your character forward.

#### 2. Event Phase
- You will encounter random events blocking your path.
- **Action**: These can take the form of typing mini-games, speed tests, or timed challenges. Successfully complete them to proceed.

#### 3. Survival Arena & Bosses
- The 3D environment transforms into the layout of your physical keyboard.
- **Movement**: Press any key to instantly teleport to its corresponding tile.
- **Combat (Spells & Skill Tree)**: Spells are not all available from the start. You must unlock and upgrade them via the **Skill Tree** accessible between runs. Once equipped, type full words to attack enemies or defend yourself:
  - `FIRE`: Launches an offensive projectile at viruses.
  - `HEAL`: Restores 15 Health Points.
  - `CIRCLE`: Summons a defensive wall of fire around you.

## 4. Technical Requirements & Troubleshooting

> [!WARNING]
> This application relies heavily on advanced 3D technologies (WebGL) to render the game world.

- **Browser**: Use a modern browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge).
- **Hardware Acceleration**: Ensure hardware acceleration is **enabled** in your browser settings to guarantee smooth 60 FPS gameplay.
- **Display Issues**: If an animation stutters or a menu freezes, simply refresh the page (`F5` or `Ctrl + F5`).
